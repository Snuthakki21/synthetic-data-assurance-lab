# Product brief

Synthetic Data Assurance Lab serves Engineering and data executives.

The decision is: Can teams test realistic relationships without copying sensitive records?

## Implemented outcome

Versioned original rules generate records; an independent auditor checks keys, domains, boundaries and precision.

## Acceptance

Compare independent integrity checks, expected category weights and repeatable seed output.

## Scope boundaries

- 50,000 rows per run; integer primary keys and acyclic references.
- No learned generator, differential privacy, warehouse connector or production benchmark.
